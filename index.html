Questions

WhatsApp Web privacy, answered.

How do I separate work and personal chats on WhatsApp Web?

Install WhatsApp Enhancer, open WhatsApp Web, and pick a space. Use the ••• button on any chat to add it to Work, Personal, or a custom space. General keeps showing every chat.

Can I lock or hide chats on WhatsApp Web?

Yes. Give any space its own 4–8 digit PIN, move chats to a Hidden vault, and turn on automatic lock after inactivity or when you switch tabs.

Does WhatsApp Enhancer read or upload my messages?

No. WhatsApp Enhancer runs only on web.whatsapp.com and stores its settings in your browser. It never collects or transmits messages, media, or contacts.

Is WhatsApp Enhancer free?

Yes. Every current feature is free. A Pro plan is in development; nothing that is free today will move behind it.
