Privacy without a hidden audience.

This policy explains how WhatsApp Enhancer handles information when you use the Chrome extension.

What WhatsApp Enhancer processes

To organize and conceal chats, WhatsApp Enhancer reads rendered chat-list labels and interface state on WhatsApp Web. It stores space names, chat-to-space mappings, hidden-chat labels, privacy preferences, theme preferences, and optional salted per-space PIN verifiers in Chrome local storage.

What WhatsApp Enhancer does not collect

WhatsApp Enhancer does not collect, transmit, sell, or share message bodies, media, contact avatars, call audio/video, PINs, browsing history, analytics, or advertising identifiers. There are no WhatsApp Enhancer servers in version 1.0.

Local storage and deletion

Settings remain in the current Chrome profile. Exported backups exclude every space PIN verifier. You can erase all extension data from Advanced settings or by uninstalling WhatsApp Enhancer.

Permissions

Storage saves local settings. Alarms supports inactivity locking while the Manifest V3 worker sleeps. Access to web.whatsapp.com is required to filter and style its rendered interface. No other website access is requested.

Chrome Web Store Limited Use

WhatsApp Enhancer’s use and transfer of information received from Google APIs complies with the Chrome Web Store User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. WhatsApp Enhancer does not transfer user data to third parties.

Security and limits

PINs are processed with PBKDF2-SHA256, a random salt, and 210,000 iterations. WhatsApp Enhancer is a privacy overlay, not account encryption or a replacement for the operating system lock. WhatsApp interface changes can temporarily reduce filtering; unknown chats fail visible.

Contact

Privacy questions can be sent through the contact method listed on the support page.
