WhatsApp Enhancer support

Get your space back on track.

A chat is missing

Open the Unsorted and Hidden views from the WhatsApp Enhancer rail. If two chats share the same visible label, WhatsApp Enhancer deliberately leaves both visible until WhatsApp exposes a safe way to distinguish them.

The rail is not visible

Refresh WhatsApp Web, then confirm WhatsApp Enhancer is enabled at chrome://extensions. Open Advanced settings and copy the adapter diagnostics when reporting the issue.

Notifications still appear

Space muting conceals rendered previews and page-level call prompts where detectable. Notifications created by WhatsApp’s service worker or the operating system may not be interceptable; disable WhatsApp desktop notifications for guaranteed silence.

Forgotten space PIN

WhatsApp Enhancer does not include a master PIN or insecure recovery questions. Remove and reinstall the extension to erase local settings, or reset extension storage from Chrome’s extension controls.

Contact

Email support@fenwicklabs.com. Include the WhatsApp Enhancer version, Chrome version, and adapter diagnostics; do not include private chat content.

Never send message contents, PINs, or private screenshots in a support request.
